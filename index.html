Whiskerbar

A cat that lives on your taskbar.

She taps her paws in time with your typing, wanders along the bottom of the screen, chases the pointer and naps when you step away. Every key you press earns a treat, and treats buy hats, breeds and furniture.

  • 12 breeds, 12 hats, glasses, collars, scarves and furniture
  • 20 achievements, a daily streak and a bond level
  • English and Hebrew, with full right-to-left layout
  • Counts how many keys you press - never which ones
  • No account, no analytics, no network connection
