Whiskerbar - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Whiskerbar is a desktop pet for Windows. It is made by Lutra Apps (Yossi Oknin). This page explains, in plain language, exactly what the app does with your information.

The short version

Whiskerbar collects nothing and sends nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no server. Everything the app knows about you stays in one folder on your own PC.

What the app counts

Whiskerbar's whole game is driven by how much you use your keyboard and mouse, so it counts two numbers: how many times a key went down, and how many times a mouse button went down.

To do that it installs a low-level Windows input hook - the same mechanism used by accessibility software and hotkey managers. Whiskerbar's hook increments a counter and nothing else. It does not read which key was pressed, does not look at the text you type, does not record mouse coordinates or window titles, and does not keep any history of your activity. It is not a keylogger and cannot be used as one.

You can pause counting at any time from the app's tray menu. While it is paused no input is observed at all.

What is stored, and where

Whiskerbar saves your game to %LOCALAPPDATA%\Whiskerbar on your own computer. The files hold:

  • your running totals of keystrokes and clicks, and your treat balance;
  • which breed, hats, accessories and furniture you have unlocked and are using;
  • your achievements, your daily streak and the dates you played;
  • your settings - language, pet size, which monitor she lives on, and so on.

These files never leave your PC. Deleting that folder, or uninstalling the app, removes all of it.

Network use

Whiskerbar has no servers and makes no network requests of its own. The one exception is the optional Whiskerbar Plus add-on: if you open the Plus page or buy it, the app asks the Microsoft Store whether you own it and what it costs. That conversation is between your PC and Microsoft, and is covered by the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Whiskerbar receives only the price and a yes/no answer about ownership - no personal details are passed to the developer.

Children

Whiskerbar is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.

אפשר להשהות את הספירה בכל רגע מתפריט המגש של האפליקציה. כשהיא מושהית לא נצפה שום קלט בכלל.

מה נשמר, ואיפה

‏Whiskerbar שומרת את המשחק שלכם בתיקייה %LOCALAPPDATA%\Whiskerbar על המחשב שלכם. הקבצים מכילים:

  • את סך ההקשות והקליקים שלכם ואת יתרת הפינוקים;
  • אילו גזע, כובעים, אביזרים ורהיטים פתחתם ואילו בשימוש;
  • את ההישגים, הרצף היומי והתאריכים שבהם שיחקתם;
  • את ההגדרות - שפה, גודל החתולה, על איזה מסך היא גרה וכדומה.

הקבצים האלה אף פעם לא עוזבים את המחשב שלכם. מחיקת התיקייה, או הסרת האפליקציה, מוחקת את הכול.
